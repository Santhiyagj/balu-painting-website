    Questions we hear often

    How long does a typical apartment take?

    Most apartments take about 4 to 6 days, depending on how much surface repair is needed. Your written quote states the schedule.

    Do I need to move my furniture?

    No. We cover and protect your furniture and floors, and we clean the site when the job is done.

    Is the quote really fixed?

    Yes. After the free site visit you get a written fixed price within 24 hours, with no hidden extras.

    Do you fix damp before painting?

    Yes. We treat leaks and seepage first, because paint over damp walls peels again.

    Is there a warranty?

    All paint work carries a 2-year warranty.
